Ningen Dock — Comprehensive Screening

About Japan Medical Concierge

JMC is a non-clinical coordination service based in Vancouver that helps Canadian residents integrate health screenings and specialist consultations into their Japan trip. We handle scheduling, document translation, travel-friendly appointment coordination, and on-ground support in Japan. Japanese hospitals independently review clinical cases and decide on care — JMC only coordinates logistics.

FAQ

Do I need Japanese health insurance?
Most travellers pay 100% self-pay for screenings and specialist visits. If you hold Japanese national insurance, let us know and we will advise.
Will JMC give medical advice?
No. JMC is a non-clinical coordinator. Hospitals and physicians in Japan review records and make medical decisions.
Can my local doctor refer a patient?
We ask local physicians to treat this as an informational option for patients; avoid language implying commercial referral relationships. Patients contact JMC directly if they wish to proceed.
