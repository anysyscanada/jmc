TRIP + HEALTH

Already planning a trip to Japan? Come back healthier too.

JMC coordinates health screenings and specialist consultations that fit into your Japan itinerary — seamless logistics, translation, and local support.

One trip, two gains: Enjoy Japan and take care of important health screenings or specialist visits while you’re there.

Services

Ningen Dock

Comprehensive, multi-day or single-day medical checkups (ningen dock) designed for early detection and health maintenance. Typical elements include blood and urine tests, ECG, chest X-ray, abdominal ultrasound, liver function tests, and physician review. Optional add-ons: advanced blood panels, tumor markers, and specialist consultations. We coordinate timing to fit your travel schedule and assist with translations and report delivery.

PET / Advanced Imaging

Scheduling for PET-CT, MRI, and other advanced imaging with faster appointment lead times than typical domestic waits. We handle pre-test instructions, arrange radiology consultations where needed, and help obtain digital copies of imaging and formal reports for your records or for review by your local physician after your trip.

Orthopedic & Specialist Consultations

Coordination of non-emergency specialist visits (orthopedics, ENT, dermatology, etc.) while you are in Japan. We collect and organize prior imaging and records, arrange interpreter-supported consultations, and follow up on next-step logistics when treatments or procedures are recommended.

FAQ

[Q] Do I need Japanese health insurance?
Most travellers pay 100% self-pay for screenings and specialist visits. If you hold Japanese national insurance, tell us and we'll advise on coverage and billing implications.
[Q] Will JMC provide medical advice or diagnoses?
No. JMC is a non-clinical coordinator. Japanese hospitals and physicians review medical records and make clinical decisions. JMC supports logistics, translation, appointment booking, and report delivery only.
[Q] How long before my trip should I contact JMC?
For routine ningen dock screenings, contact us 4–8 weeks before travel. For specialist consultations or imaging (PET, MRI), contact us as early as 8–12 weeks to secure preferred appointment times.
[Q] What documents do I need to prepare?
Bring any recent medical records, imaging (CD or digital files), medication lists, and your passport. We can assist with secure electronic transfer of records to Japanese hospitals when needed.
[Q] Are interpreter services provided?
Yes. We provide interpreter support (Japanese-English-Chinese) for appointments and report explanations when requested.
[Q] Can local Canadian doctors refer patients through JMC?
We ask local clinicians to present JMC as an informational option to patients rather than as a commercial referral. Patients should contact JMC directly if they want assistance—this avoids regulatory and liability concerns.
