Services

Ningen Dock

Comprehensive, multi-day or single-day medical checkups (ningen dock) designed for early detection and health maintenance. Typical elements include blood and urine tests, ECG, chest X-ray, abdominal ultrasound, liver function tests, and physician review. Optional add-ons: advanced blood panels, tumor markers, and specialist consultations. We coordinate timing to fit your travel schedule and assist with translations and report delivery.

PET / Advanced Imaging

Scheduling for PET-CT, MRI, and other advanced imaging with faster appointment lead times than typical domestic waits. We handle pre-test instructions, arrange radiology consultations where needed, and help obtain digital copies of imaging and formal reports for your records or for review by your local physician after your trip.

Orthopedic & Specialist Consultations

Coordination of non-emergency specialist visits (orthopedics, orthopedics surgery consults, ENT, dermatology, etc.) while you are in Japan. We collect and organize prior imaging and records, arrange interpreter-supported consultations, and follow up on next-step logistics when treatments or procedures are recommended.

FAQ

Do I need Japanese health insurance?
Most travellers pay 100% self-pay for screenings and specialist visits. If you hold Japanese national insurance, let us know and we will advise.
Will JMC give medical advice?
No. JMC is a non-clinical coordinator. Hospitals and physicians in Japan review records and make medical decisions.
Can my local doctor refer a patient?
We ask local physicians to treat this as an informational option for patients; avoid language implying commercial referral relationships. Patients contact JMC directly if they wish to proceed.
